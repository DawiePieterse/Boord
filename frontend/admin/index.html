Set up your farm
This server is running, but it does not know whose farm it is yet. Nine short steps and it will.
Step 1 of 9 Optional
Whose farm is this?
The farm name appears in every screen header, on picking slips and on every report. The supplier name is how the farm's own fruit is labelled where it sits alongside other growers' deliveries.
Starts as "Own Farm". Anything you pack for other growers gets its own supplier later.
Where is it?
Every crate is stamped with the weather at the moment it was captured, and the Risk indicator and Harvest Forecast are built from this location's history. Boord will not guess at coordinates: weather for the wrong place looks completely normal and quietly feeds numbers about somewhere else.
What do you pay per kilogram?
There is no default rate, and there will not be one. A wrong block list is obvious the moment somebody opens the Field app; a wrong wage rate produces a perfectly ordinary-looking payslip at the wrong number, and the person most likely to notice is the one being underpaid. Until a rate exists, Payments refuses to calculate at all.
You can change this any time under Settings - rates are kept as a dated history, so past wage sheets keep the rate they were calculated at.
How long may fruit sit in transit?
The pack house queue is ordered oldest-first and colour-coded by how long a load has been waiting. These are the two boundaries. The defaults suit most farms - change them if your run to the pack house is unusually long or short.
Your orchard blocks
A block register is the shape of one particular orchard - its labels, varieties, tree counts and hectares - so a new install starts with none. Until these are loaded the Field app has nothing to pick from.

Download blocks.csv - columns: id, name, variety, trees, hectares, active

Your people
Load the workforce now or add people one at a time under Master Data - either works. These records hold ID numbers and bank details, so they never leave the server and are never served to an unauthenticated device.
Columns: emp_nr, first_name, last_name, id_number, bank, account, whatsapp_number, supplier_id, active
.csv or .xlsx
Name your stations
Eight device slots are ready. Rename them to the places they will actually stand, so a picking slip says where it came from. Each tablet picks its slot once, from this list, the first time it opens the app.
Seasons before Boord
Optional, and easy to do later. Past harvests are what the Analysis tab compares this season against, and what the Risk indicator and Harvest Forecast are fitted to - without them those screens work, they just have nothing to measure against yet.
Daily harvest records
One row per block per day. Download the template - columns: block_id, date, kg, estimated.
Season totals, further back
One row per season, block optional. Download the template - columns: season_year, kg, block_id, estimated.
Weather history
Downloads every hour of weather at the location you entered. The Risk indicator scores each season against the weather that season had, so this needs to reach back as far as your own harvest figures do - but every extra year is another ~8,700 hours to download, and it needs internet the whole way.
Each import replaces everything previously loaded into that table rather than adding to it, so a corrected sheet can simply be imported again.

Keeping it up to date
Run update_server.bat on this PC to install new releases. Backups run by themselves every night at 02:00 and the last 14 are kept - see Settings.
Everything here also lives under Settings and Master Data - nothing is decided permanently on this screen.
